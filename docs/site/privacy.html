Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

In short: DashCast has no account, no ads, no tracking and no analytics. We do not collect your personal data. What you add to the app stays on your iPhone.

Who we are

DashCast is an iPhone app made by an independent developer. Contact: alexbh987@gmail.com.

What we do not do

  • No account or sign-up.
  • No advertising and no ad identifiers.
  • No tracking across apps or websites.
  • No analytics or crash-reporting services that send data off your device.
  • We run no server that receives your data, and we do not sell or share data with anyone.

Data that stays on your iPhone

  • Playlists and sources: playlist links, imported playlist files, favourites and watch history are stored in the app on your device.
  • Media server details: the Jellyfin or Emby server address and user name you enter are stored on your device. Access tokens for those servers are stored in the iOS Keychain.
  • Trips: trip routes, distance, speed and scores are stored on your device only.
  • Widgets and settings: your widget designs, car photo and preferences are stored on your device and shared only with DashCast's own Home Screen widgets.

If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it. Data may be included in your own iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), which Apple handles under its own privacy policy.

Permissions and why they are used

  • Location: used on your device to show speed, map your trips and, if you allow it, detect trips automatically. Location is never uploaded.
  • Motion & Fitness: used on your device to tell when you are driving, so DashCast can record trips and keep video parked-only on a car display. Never uploaded.
  • Camera: used only to scan a QR code that contains a playlist link. No photos or video are taken or stored.
  • Photos: used only when you choose a video or photo to play, show on the car screen or use in a widget. DashCast does not browse or upload your library.
  • Local network: used to reach a media server on your home network and, if you start screen mirroring, to show your screen on a car display on the same network.

You can change these permissions at any time in iOS Settings → DashCast.

Network connections the app makes

DashCast connects directly from your iPhone to the playlists, streams, media servers and web pages that you add or open. Those services are run by their owners, not by us, and they may see your IP address and requests as with any internet connection. Their own privacy policies apply.

Purchases

The DashCast Pro purchase is handled by Apple through the App Store (StoreKit). We do not receive your payment details or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy applies to purchases.

Children

DashCast does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.

Contact

Questions about privacy: alexbh987@gmail.com.
