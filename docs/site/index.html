DashCast

Your own playlists, media server and videos on the car screen, for passengers and parked breaks. Pay once, keep it forever.

  • Plays M3U / M3U8 playlists you add from a link, a file or a QR code
  • Connects to your own Jellyfin or Emby server
  • Plays videos you pick from Photos or Files
  • Sends video to a connected display (AirPlay, HDMI or a compatible car screen) and keeps your iPhone as the remote
  • Trip stats, car widgets and an arrival sound board

No account. No tracking.

There is no sign-up, no ads and no analytics. Your playlists, server logins and trips stay on your iPhone. Read the Privacy Policy.

Pay once

DashCast is free to download. One in-app purchase, DashCast Pro, unlocks everything for good. There is no subscription.

Bring your own content

DashCast ships with no channels or videos. You add sources you have the right to watch. See the Terms of Use.

Safety: video is for passengers, or for when the vehicle is parked. Never watch video while driving, and follow local laws about screens the driver can see.
