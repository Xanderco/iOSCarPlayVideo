Direct Cast & Screen Mirroring

Cast anything to your car

Connect your iPhone to CarPlay or an external car display, open DashCast, and enjoy seamless playback on the vehicle screen.

  • 1
    Apps with a Cast button

    In compatible video apps, tap the Cast button and choose your car. DashCast receives high-definition streams and renders them directly on the car display.

  • 2
    Any other app via Screen Mirroring

    Mirror your complete iPhone display to the car with low-latency H.264 video. Open any video, photo album, or social app and it appears crisp and fluid on your dash.

  • 3
    Keep watching in the background

    Playback runs effortlessly with a Live Activity on your Lock Screen and Dynamic Island, letting you switch apps or lock your phone while video continues on the car screen.
