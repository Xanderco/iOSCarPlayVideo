# DashCast — Official Promotional Video Storyboard & Production Script [DI-13]

## Target Formats & Specifications
- **Platform**: TikTok, Instagram Reels, YouTube Shorts
- **Aspect Ratio**: 9:16 Vertical (1080 x 1920 or 1206 x 2622)
- **Target Runtime**: 60 Seconds (High-Retention Edit) / 90 Seconds (Extended Deep-Dive)
- **Color Palette**: Electric Racing Yellow (`#FFE600`), Deep OLED Black (`#0C0D10`), and Vibrant Gold (`#FFB800`)
- **Tone**: High-energy, automotive enthusiast, premium tech, disruptive value

---

## The Core Value Proposition & Disruption
> **"Why pay $9.99 every single month just to watch YouTube or customize your car screen? DashCast gives you Video Cast, Live Drive Telemetry, Custom CarPlay Widgets, and Supercar Startup Sounds for ONE single lifetime purchase ($19.99). Pay once. Own forever. Zero subscriptions."**

## 🎬 60-Second Viral Production Script

### Scene 1: The High-Stakes Hook (0:00 – 0:06)
- **Visual**: Quick zoom into a dark parking garage. Driver is sitting inside a sports car or modern EV (e.g. BMW M4 or Porsche 911). Cockpit ambient LEDs glow in Electric Yellow. Center console display is showing Apple CarPlay.
- **Action**: Host turns to camera, taps the car steering wheel.
- **On-Screen Text**: `STOP PAYING $10/MO FOR CARPLAY APPS 🛑`
- **Voiceover**: *"If your car has Apple CarPlay, you need to stop scrolling right now. You’ve been lied to."*
- **SFX**: Deep sub-bass drop + high-rev engine roar.

---

### Scene 2: The Universal Pain Point (0:06 – 0:14)
- **Visual**: Cut to close-up of CarPlay screen showing the standard boring grid of icons (Maps, Messages, Podcasts). Cut to a competitor app showing an obnoxious "$9.99 / Month" subscription paywall.
- **Action**: Host shakes their head in frustration.
- **On-Screen Text**: `No Subscriptions. No $150 Android Boxes. No Jailbreak.`
- **Voiceover**: *"Apple won't let you watch video while you’re waiting in your car, and other apps charge you an annoying monthly subscription just to mirror your phone."*
- **SFX**: Buzzer / record scratch.

---

### Scene 3: The Magic Moment — Parked 1080p Video Casting (0:14 – 0:28)
- **Visual**: Host holds up their iPhone running DashCast in Electric Racing Yellow theme. One single tap on `Browser` -> taps `YouTube` (or selects an M3U livestream of a live soccer match).
- **Action**: Seamless whip-pan to the wide car dashboard. The video pops up instantly in crisp 1080p, perfectly formatted to the car's screen width. Audio pumps cleanly through the car speakers.
- **On-Screen Text**: `📺 1080p Video on Your Car Display`
- **Voiceover**: *"Meet DashCast. While you're parked, charging your EV, or waiting for someone, one tap casts YouTube, Twitch, Netflix, or your personal videos straight to your car's screen in full 1080p."*
- **SFX**: Smooth electronic chime + automotive startup synth.

---

### Scene 4: More Than Video — Complete In-Car Suite (0:28 – 0:44)
- **Visual Fast Cuts (3 seconds each)**:
  1. **Custom CarPlay Widgets (0:28 – 0:34)**:
     - Cut to CarPlay dashboard showing a custom widget with the driver's exact car render (`Porsche 911 GT3 RS`), customized license plate `ABC-1234`, and weather.
     - *On-Screen Text*: `🧩 Custom CarPlay Dashboard Widgets`
  2. **Automotive Startup Sounds (0:34 – 0:39)**:
     - Host plugs in phone or enters car: A glorious V12 twin-turbo spool or "Welcome Back Sir" chime plays automatically through the car sound system.
     - *On-Screen Text*: `🔊 Supercar Startup Sounds & Chimes`
  3. **Live Drive Telemetry & Smoothness Score (0:39 – 0:44)**:
     - Cut to the digital neon speedometer and GPS route map showing driving score `96/100`.
     - *On-Screen Text*: `⚡ Speedometer HUD & Drive Scoring`
- **Voiceover**: *"And it doesn't stop there. You get custom CarPlay widgets with your exact car model, supercar engine startup sounds when you plug in, and a live drive telemetry HUD."*
- **SFX**: Turbo spool, rev limiter pop, crisp UI feedback clicks.

---

### Scene 5: The Disruption & The Offer (0:44 – 0:54)
- **Visual**: Split screen comparing Competitor vs. DashCast:
  - *Left (Competitor)*: Red text `$9.99/mo` ($120 every year forever)
  - *Right (DashCast)*: Electric Yellow text `$19.99 LIFETIME (Pay Once, Own Forever)`
- **On-Screen Text**: `DashCast: Pay Once ($19.99) · Own Forever 👑`
- **Voiceover**: *"The best part? Zero monthly fees. No subscriptions ever. Pay once for a lifetime pass, and you own everything forever."*
- **SFX**: Cash register chime / celebratory win sound.

---

### Scene 6: The Call to Action (0:54 – 1:00)
- **Visual**: High-energy hero shot: The car pulling out of the garage, phone docked on MagSafe mount glowing with the yellow DashCast interface. App Store badge and DashCast AppIcon animate into center frame.
- **On-Screen Text**: `Download DashCast on the App Store 🚗💨`
- **Voiceover**: *"Search DashCast on the App Store or tap the link in bio to unlock your car today."*
- **SFX**: High-tempo music swell, crisp final bass thump.
