### Checked and OK

- Privacy manifest: no tracking. Required-reason APIs declared: UserDefaults `CA92.1` and file timestamp `C617.1`. No disk-space, `systemUptime` or keyboard APIs are used.
- The camera is requested and denial is handled (`PlaylistQRImportView`). Location has When-In-Use plus Always strings. Background location is enabled only while recording.
- `WebVideoExtractor` uses a weak script-message proxy (no `WKUserContentController` retain cycle).
- No duplicate playlist stores, players or design-token sets: one `PlaylistStore`, one `VideoPlayerService`, `DS*`/`dc*` tokens, and v1 `dash*` colours kept as deprecated aliases on purpose.
- Force unwraps: only `layer as! AVPlayerLayer` (guaranteed by `layerClass`), literal sample URLs, and `MediaServerClient.endpoint` `comps.url!` (path always starts with `/`). All safe.
